2. About This App and Its Users

MillyLog is designed for parents and caregivers to document nursing and feeding sessions. This app is directed at parents and caregivers, not at children. All data entered into the app — including baby profiles — is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Data Collected

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Timestamps of nursing and feeding sessions
  • Notes about meals
  • Baby profiles (name, date of birth, gender, profile picture)
  • Exported JSON/CSV files (only when manually exported by you)

We do not collect, sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any third party. Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing for us to sell or share.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MillyLog is not directed at children under the age of 13. While the app allows parents to create profiles for their babies, it does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. All data is entered by parents or caregivers and stored solely on their device. If you believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us at millylog@posteo.eu so we can assist you.

5. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal data is collected about you. As MillyLog stores all data exclusively on your device and does not transmit, sell, or share any data with third parties, there is no personal data held by us that could be subject to a CCPA request. For any questions, please contact millylog@posteo.eu.

6. Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The app contains no analytics tools, advertising, or tracking mechanisms.

7. Retention Period

Data is stored for as long as the app is installed on your device or until you delete it manually. Please note that exports or device backups you create (e.g. iCloud backups) are not automatically affected by this.

8. Your Rights

You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app. When exporting, you have full control over your data.

9. Medical Disclaimer

The content and features of this app are intended solely for personal documentation of nursing and feeding sessions. MillyLog does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any questions or concerns about your or your baby's health. Do not disregard or delay seeking professional medical advice based on information recorded in this app.
