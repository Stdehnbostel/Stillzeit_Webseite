Privacy Policy

1. Provider of the App

Stefan Wagner
Email: millylog@posteo.eu

2. Data Collection and Processing

MillyLog stores all entered data (nursing times, meals, notes) exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

3. Data Stored

  • Timestamps of nursing and feeding sessions
  • Notes about meals
  • Baby profiles (name, date of birth, gender, profile picture)
  • Exported JSON/CSV files (only when manually exported by you)

This data may constitute health data within the meaning of applicable data protection law.

4. No Processing by the Provider

All data entered in the app is stored exclusively on your device. The provider does not receive, collect, store, or process any personal data from the app and has no access to it whatsoever. No data is transmitted to the provider or to any third party.

Because the provider does not process any personal data from the app, the provider is not a "controller" within the meaning of Art. 4(7) UK GDPR / EU GDPR in this respect, and no legal basis is required. Any processing of the data you enter is carried out solely by you on your own device and generally falls within the household exemption (Art. 2(2)(c) GDPR).

5. Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The app contains no analytics tools, advertising, or tracking mechanisms.

6. Retention Period

Data is stored for as long as the app is installed on your device or until you delete it manually. Please note that exports or device backups you create (e.g. iCloud backups) are not automatically affected by this.

7. Your Rights

You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app. When exporting, you have full control over your data. Under applicable data protection law (UK GDPR / EU GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20)
  • Right to object (Art. 21)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77)

8. Medical Disclaimer

The content and features of this app are intended solely for personal documentation of nursing and feeding sessions. MillyLog does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any questions or concerns about your or your baby's health.
